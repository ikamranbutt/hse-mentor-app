HSE Mentor Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 August 2026

HSE Mentor is an educational health, safety and environment learning application. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information handled by the app

HSE Mentor does not require an account and does not ask for your name, address, phone number or payment information. Lesson progress, exam progress, reading timers and preferences are stored locally on your device so you can continue where you stopped.

Microphone and speech features

The mock interview feature may request microphone permission when you choose to answer by voice. Audio is used to convert your speech into text through speech-recognition services available on your device or browser. HSE Mentor does not operate a server that stores your recordings. You can deny microphone access and use available text controls instead.

Files you create

Risk assessments and other documents that you generate are created for you to download or save. HSE Mentor does not upload these documents to an HSE Mentor server.

Data sharing and selling

HSE Mentor does not sell personal data. The app does not include an HSE Mentor advertising or analytics service in this release. Your device, browser or speech-recognition provider may process technical information under its own privacy terms.

Data retention and deletion

Locally saved app progress remains on your device until you clear the app data, clear browser storage or uninstall the app. Because HSE Mentor does not maintain user accounts or a user database, there is no separate server account to delete.

Children

HSE Mentor is designed for workplace safety learners and professionals. It is not directed to children under 13.

Educational disclaimer

HSE Mentor is an independent educational tool. It does not replace official training, certification, legal requirements, company procedures or competent professional advice.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features change. The effective date shown above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact details displayed on the HSE Mentor Google Play listing.
