## Full description

Prepare for HSE and Safety Officer interviews while building practical workplace safety knowledge with HSE Mentor.

HSE Mentor is a structured health, safety and environment learning app for Safety Officers, HSE Officers, supervisors, engineers, students and professionals. Progress from foundation topics to advanced HSE management through focused lessons, timed exams, practical scenarios, mock interviews and professional risk assessment tools.

LEARN HEALTH AND SAFETY

• Study 20 structured HSE modules with 200 focused lessons
• Learn hazard identification, risk assessment and the hierarchy of controls
• Cover permit to work, PPE, toolbox talks and safe systems of work
• Explore work at height, scaffolding, lifting and rigging, hot work and excavation
• Review confined space entry, electrical safety, LOTO, fire safety and emergency response
• Build knowledge of incident investigation, occupational health and HSE management systems

PRACTICE HSE INTERVIEWS

• Start with basic Safety Officer interview questions
• Progress to advanced, scenario-based HSE interview practice
• Answer by voice in the live mock interview experience
• Receive feedback on key safety points and improve your answer structure
• Practice realistic situations involving hazards, controls and on-site decisions

TAKE EXAMS AND TRACK PROGRESS

• Complete lesson practice and module assessments
• Use one-question-at-a-time exam mode with a 60-minute timer
• Review scores, correct and incorrect totals, and module progress
• Continue lesson reading time from where you stopped

APPLY SAFETY KNOWLEDGE

• Work through animated practical safety scenarios
• Select suitable control measures and identify unsafe choices
• Create professional risk assessments with activity, hazard, effect, risk rating, controls, residual risk and responsible party
• Export risk assessments for practical use

HSE Mentor supports practical learning for construction, industrial and general workplace safety. Use it for daily HSE learning, Safety Officer interview preparation, refresher study and professional development.

Important: HSE Mentor is an independent educational tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by OSHA, NEBOSH, ISO or any government authority. App content does not replace official training, certification, legal requirements, site procedures or competent professional advice.
